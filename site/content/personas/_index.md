Continuous assurance spans multiple organizational roles. To streamline adoption and operational efficiency, OSKAL organizes guidance into dedicated Persona Journeys.

## Select Your Role

1. **[Security Officer & Compliance Auditor](/personas/security-officer-auditor/)**:
   Focus on NIST SP 800-53 controls, FedRAMP continuous assessment, non-repudiation receipts, Section 51 Explain Graph verification, and elimination of fake compliance claims.

2. **[Platform Engineer & Kubernetes SRE](/personas/platform-sre/)**:
   Focus on cluster architecture, CRD deployment, controller reconciliation, low-latency CEL admission, Tetragon/Cilium integration, and drift recovery.

3. **[Workload & AI Developer](/personas/workload-ai-developer/)**:
   Focus on meeting evidence contracts, signing container images with Cosign, attaching AI-BOMs/SHIELD manifests, and utilizing the `pkg/oskal` Go SDK.

4. **[SecOps & Risk Analyst](/personas/secops-risk-analyst/)**:
   Focus on real-time drift triage, vector state analysis, bounded derogations via `ControlException`, and forensic audit trail reconstruction.
