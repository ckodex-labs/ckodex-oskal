How-To Guides are problem-oriented and recipe-focused. They assume basic familiarity with OSKAL and provide precise procedures to accomplish concrete operational tasks across Kubernetes clusters, supply chains, and compliance audits.

## Key Operational Tasks

- Binding Kubernetes ValidatingAdmissionPolicies (CEL) to NIST controls.
- Enforcing zero-trust SPIFFE/SPIRE SVID authority validation.
- Verifying container images and AI model weights with Sigstore and Cosign.
- Exporting attested state to NIST OSCAL 1.2.3 formats.
- Detecting workload generation changes and recovering from epoch drift.
- Running the Dagger SSDLC pipeline with Syft SBOM and Grype vulnerability scanning.
